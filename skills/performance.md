# Performance Skill

- **Purpose:** Establish representative budgets, capacity evidence, and regression controls.
- **When to use:** Performance is a stated quality attribute or a material architecture risk.
- **When NOT to use:** Micro-optimisation without a baseline or outcome impact.
- **Inputs:** Journeys, workload, SLOs, topology, telemetry, cost and scale.
- **Outputs:** Model, benchmark/load tests, profiles, capacity, recommendation.
- **Dependencies:** Stable environment and observable system.
- **Example:** Measure p50/p95/p99, errors, saturation, and cost across a ramp and soak profile.

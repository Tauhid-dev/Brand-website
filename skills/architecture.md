# Architecture Skill

- **Purpose:** Design boundaries, dependencies, quality scenarios, alternatives, and ADRs.
- **When to use:** Material structure, integration, deployment, data, or compatibility decisions.
- **When NOT to use:** Cosmetic refactoring with no contract or quality impact.
- **Inputs:** Requirements, domain, constraints, research, risks, quality attributes.
- **Outputs:** Views, rules, options, decisions, migration, rollback, review triggers.
- **Dependencies:** Planning, domain-modelling, security, diagramming.
- **Example:** Compare synchronous and asynchronous integration against latency and recovery needs.

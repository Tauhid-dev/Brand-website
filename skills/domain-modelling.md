# Domain Modelling Skill

- **Purpose:** Discover language, contexts, invariants, aggregates, policies, and events.
- **When to use:** Complex rules, ambiguous language, multiple owners, or consistency decisions.
- **When NOT to use:** A simple CRUD/data-transfer domain with no meaningful behavior.
- **Inputs:** Workflows, stakeholder language, requirements, business rules, failures.
- **Outputs:** Glossary, context map, model, invariants, events, open questions.
- **Dependencies:** Planning artifacts and domain experts.
- **Example:** Split two meanings of “account” into owned contexts with an explicit translation.

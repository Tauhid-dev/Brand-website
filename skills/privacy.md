# Privacy Skill

- **Purpose:** Minimise and govern personal data through purpose, access, retention, and deletion.
- **When to use:** Personal, behavioural, location, identity, biometric, or sensitive data may be processed.
- **When NOT to use:** As a substitute for qualified jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
- **Inputs:** Data flow, purpose, actors, locations, vendors, retention, rights.
- **Outputs:** Inventory, minimisation, authority questions, controls, risk and review.
- **Dependencies:** Security and legal/governance authority.
- **Example:** Remove an unnecessary identifier and define deletion propagation to processors and backups.

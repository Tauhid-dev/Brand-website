# Testing Skill

- **Purpose:** Design efficient, deterministic evidence for requirements and risks.
- **When to use:** Requirement, contract, behavior, migration, control, or quality change.
- **When NOT to use:** To inflate coverage with assertions unrelated to risk.
- **Inputs:** Acceptance, architecture, threats, failure modes, targets.
- **Outputs:** Strategy, cases, fixtures, automation, results, coverage gaps.
- **Dependencies:** Stable contracts and representative environment/data.
- **Example:** Pair property tests for an invariant with adapter contract and recovery tests.

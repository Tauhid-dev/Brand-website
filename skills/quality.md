# Quality Skill

- **Purpose:** Define measurable entry/exit gates, evidence, ownership, and waivers.
- **When to use:** Bootstrap, chunk completion, merge, migration, release, resume.
- **When NOT to use:** To replace engineering judgment with a generic checklist.
- **Inputs:** Risks, requirements, test strategy, delivery policy, authorities.
- **Outputs:** Gates, thresholds, evidence register, waiver and escalation paths.
- **Dependencies:** Validation and accountable owners.
- **Example:** Block release until rollback rehearsal meets RTO or an expiring risk waiver is approved.

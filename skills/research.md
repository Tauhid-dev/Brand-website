# Research Skill

- **Purpose:** Answer decision-relevant questions with current, attributable evidence.
- **When to use:** Facts are uncertain, current, niche, regulated, costly, or decision-critical.
- **When NOT to use:** The answer is already authoritative in repository contracts.
- **Inputs:** Precise question, decision, scope, acceptable sources, review date.
- **Outputs:** Sources, findings, contradictions, inference, confidence, recommendation.
- **Dependencies:** Source access and domain reviewer where risk warrants.
- **Example:** Compare primary vendor guarantees against required RTO, noting gaps and expiry.

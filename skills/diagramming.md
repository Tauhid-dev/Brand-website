# Diagramming Skill

- **Purpose:** Communicate scoped architecture relationships as versioned Mermaid views.
- **When to use:** Three or more elements/relationships, boundaries, workflows, state, or deployment.
- **When NOT to use:** A sentence or small table communicates the relationship more clearly.
- **Inputs:** Authoritative model, audience, scope, notation, relationship semantics.
- **Outputs:** Titled Mermaid source, labelled edges, legend/context, SVG derivative.
- **Dependencies:** Architecture model and diagram validator.
- **Example:** Show containers and protocols without mixing component or class detail.

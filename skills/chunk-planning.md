# Chunk Planning Skill

- **Purpose:** Turn a roadmap into bounded, dependency-ordered, context-free work packets.
- **When to use:** Before execution or when scope/dependencies materially change.
- **When NOT to use:** To conceal an unresolved architecture or authority decision.
- **Inputs:** Requirements, architecture, risks, roadmap, skills, quality gates.
- **Outputs:** Chunk DAG and complete chunk contracts with evidence and rollback.
- **Dependencies:** Planning, architecture, validation.
- **Example:** Separate schema expansion from adoption and later contraction with explicit compatibility gates.

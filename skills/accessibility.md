# Accessibility Skill

- **Purpose:** Verify inclusive outcomes across semantics, input, perception, comprehension, and assistive technology.
- **When to use:** Any human-facing channel, content, document, or interaction.
- **When NOT to use:** As an automated-only final check.
- **Inputs:** Journeys, target standard, supported technologies, design/content.
- **Outputs:** Requirements, automated/manual results, findings, remediation.
- **Dependencies:** User/channel definition and accessibility reviewer.
- **Example:** Validate keyboard order, focus recovery, errors, zoom, contrast, and screen-reader flow.

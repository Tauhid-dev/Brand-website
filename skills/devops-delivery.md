# DevOps and Delivery Skill

- **Purpose:** Design secure reproducible build, promotion, deployment, observation, and recovery.
- **When to use:** Pipeline, environment, infrastructure, configuration, or release changes.
- **When NOT to use:** To select a platform before requirements and constraints justify it.
- **Inputs:** Architecture, SLOs, gates, controls, artifact and environment model.
- **Outputs:** Pipeline, provenance, deployment/rollback, runbooks, telemetry, evidence.
- **Dependencies:** Security, testing, quality, operations ownership.
- **Example:** Promote one signed artifact through canary with abort metrics and rehearsed rollback.

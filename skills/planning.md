# Planning Skill

- **Purpose:** Translate intent into measurable outcomes, scope, requirements, acceptance, and roadmap.
- **When to use:** Bootstrap, discovery, scope change, milestone planning.
- **When NOT to use:** Implementing an already approved bounded chunk.
- **Inputs:** Brief, stakeholders, constraints, evidence, risks.
- **Outputs:** Vision, requirement traceability, acceptance, priorities, questions.
- **Dependencies:** Constitution, research skill, accountable product authority.
- **Example:** Convert “reduce handling time” into baseline, target, actors, workflow, constraints, and testable scenarios.

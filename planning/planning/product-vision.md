# Product Vision — Zuno Pixel

## Problem

A production-quality Australian local-business growth platform with a public marketing site, commercial backend, customer account experience, internal administration and future agent-platform integration.

## Intended outcome

Deliver a safe, maintainable capability that measurably addresses the stated
problem while respecting the declared constraints.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Need | Evidence of value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary users | Complete the core outcome reliably | Agreed outcome metric and user validation |
| Product owner | Prioritise scope and risk | Accepted milestones and traceability |
| Operators | Operate, recover, and diagnose safely | SLOs, runbooks, alerts, and rollback evidence |
| Governance stakeholders | Demonstrate compliance | Decision, security, privacy, and test evidence |

## Constraints

- WCAG 2.2 AA and strong Core Web Vitals
- Australian English, AUD pricing plus GST, and ethical review practices
- Central configuration must support a future rebrand
- Forms must be honest, privacy-preserving, validated, and adapter-based
- Use a modular monolith with explicit domain and application service boundaries
- Customer, onboarding, billing, notification and agent-provisioning lifecycles remain separate and auditable

## Non-goals

- Technology selection before requirements and quality attributes are understood.
- Features that do not trace to an approved outcome.
- Commitments that depend on undocumented conversation context.

## Success measures

- Outcome metrics have named owners, baselines, and target dates.
- All accepted requirements map to evidence.
- Security, privacy, accessibility, operability, and performance gates pass.
